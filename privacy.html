Privacy policy

Last updated 30 August 2026. Applies to Maturity for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

The short version. Maturity has no developer-operated server, analytics or account system. Your records stay on your devices and sync only through your private iCloud container. The developer cannot see them. The optional exchange-rate feature sends currency codes but no portfolio records. Exports are created locally only when you ask, and any recipient you choose has its own privacy terms.

1. What data the app holds

Maturity stores only what you type into it. That includes:

  • Institution and account names, and optional account or certificate references
  • Deposit amounts, interest rates, dates, payment frequencies and day-count bases
  • Cash transactions, including opening balances, deposits, interest, maturities, early breaks, fees, transfers and reconciliation adjustments
  • Notes and transaction memos

The app does not ask for your name, email address, date of birth, location, contacts, photos, banking credentials, advertising identifier or device identifier.

If indicative conversion is enabled, the app also caches public exchange rates, their publication dates and the service's supported-currency list on that device. Disabling the feature deletes this fetched cache.

2. Where that data lives

Records are stored locally in the app's container. When you are signed into iCloud with iCloud Drive available, they also sync through a private CloudKit container associated with your Apple Account.

Apple operates that storage under Apple's privacy policy. Maturity's developer has no credentials or mechanism for reading, exporting or recovering its contents. Without iCloud, the app continues to work with local storage only.

3. What is not collected

  • No analytics or telemetry. The App Store app reports no usage events to the developer.
  • No tracking. No advertising identifiers, fingerprinting, cross-app or cross-site tracking, or data-broker sharing.
  • No third-party SDKs in the app. The App Store binary uses Apple's frameworks. The separately built command-line and MCP tools described in section 7 are not part of the app binary.
  • No developer-operated crash service. If you enable Apple's system diagnostic sharing, Apple may provide anonymised diagnostics under your Apple settings.
  • No advertising. The app shows none.

For App Store purposes, the intended privacy answer is Data Not Collected because the developer does not receive this data.

4. No connection to your bank

Maturity never requests banking credentials and never connects to a financial institution, uses open banking, scrapes an account or moves money. Every financial record is entered manually. The optional rate service supplies public reference data only; it cannot see bank accounts or transact with them.

5. Network activity

The app can make network connections for two purposes:

  1. Private iCloud sync. Records sync through Apple's CloudKit service to your own private container.
  2. Optional published exchange rates. If indicative conversion is enabled, the app may retrieve Frankfurter's public supported-currency list and request rates using ISO currency codes, for example ?base=HKD&symbols=AUD,USD.

Rate requests contain no amounts, balances, institution names, record dates, notes, references, record identifiers, API key or app-generated user identifier. Frankfurter and its hosting infrastructure can see the request and ordinary network metadata such as the source IP address and standard HTTP/TLS headers, and may process operational logs under their own practices. The developer does not receive those logs. Leave indicative conversion disabled if you do not want this connection.

Fetched rates are held locally for offline estimates. They are never written into a deposit, ledger entry, balance, interest calculation or yield. Converted totals are visibly approximate and dated. Disabling the feature removes the fetched cache.

6. User-triggered exports

Maturity can create a versioned JSON analysis snapshot or a document package containing separate CSV tables. Exporting is entirely local and occurs only after you open the Export screen and press Export. The app does not automatically upload an export, send it to an AI provider or expose its SwiftData store to another process.

Three privacy choices are available:

  • Complete includes institution and account names, references, notes and transaction memos.
  • Financial analysis replaces institution names with aliases and excludes references, notes and memos; account names remain.
  • Redacted, the default, replaces institution and account names with export-specific aliases and excludes all user-entered free text.

Aliases are consistent within one artifact but change when a new export is generated. Amounts, rates, dates, currencies and record relationship identifiers remain so the artifact can be analysed and its tables joined accurately. Review an export before sharing it, particularly when choosing Complete or Financial analysis.

On Mac, the system save panel writes only to a location you select. On iPhone and iPad, the system share sheet controls the destination. Any recipient or destination operates under its own privacy terms. Temporary share files are removed when the flow ends; files left by an interrupted process are removed the next time the Export screen opens.

7. Optional local analysis tools

The Maturity source repository provides separately built command-line and Model Context Protocol (MCP) tools for validating and reading an export. These tools are not part of, launched by or registered by the App Store app. They cannot open Maturity's SwiftData store, private iCloud container or other live app data. They operate only on a JSON export explicitly supplied by the user.

The MCP server is read-only. It can validate an embedded export and filter summary, deposit, scheduled-cashflow, maturity-ladder, liquidity and attention rows already in that artifact. It has no filesystem browsing, export-path access, network transport, telemetry, record mutation or financial-action tools. It cannot create or edit deposits, record transactions, move money or send instructions back to the app. The complete technical boundary is documented on the Docs page.

The local tools do not upload exports by themselves. However, when you supply an export to an MCP client, AI assistant or other analysis application, that client or its service provider receives the artifact and may transmit, process, retain or log it under its own terms. Prefer Redacted exports, inspect the artifact first and use only tools and providers you trust. User-directed sharing does not give Maturity's developer access to the export.

8. Deleting your data

  • Individual records. Delete deposits or transactions inside the app.
  • Local copy. Delete the app to remove its local container from that device.
  • iCloud copy. Remove Maturity's data through the device's iCloud storage settings to delete the synced copy.

Deleting the app alone may not remove its iCloud copy. Because the developer has no copy, there is no developer-side account or dataset to delete on request.

9. Children

Maturity is a personal finance record-keeping tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from them.

10. Your rights

The developer does not hold user records. You control records directly through the app and your iCloud settings. Privacy rights concerning data processed by Apple are governed by your relationship with Apple. Data you separately provide to an MCP client, AI assistant or other recipient is governed by that recipient's terms and controls.

11. Not financial advice

Maturity is a record-keeping and calculation tool. It does not provide financial, investment or taxation advice or recommend products or institutions. Bank statements and quotes remain authoritative.

12. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be published on this page with a revised date and reflected in the App Store privacy information before the corresponding release.
